Secure Wallet & Payments
  • Wallet funding via trusted payment providers (e.g., Paystack)
  • Clear separation between wallet funds and participation tickets

Transparent Participation & Selection
  • Digital tickets provide access to participation experiences
  • Tickets are not money and have no cash value
  • Each ticket grants entry into a defined event
  • Transparent ticket rules displayed upfront
  • System-based selection processes
  • Pre-declared participation rules
  • Fairness and integrity controls
  • Final outcomes subject to verification
Non-Cash Rewards
  • Rewards are non-monetary
  • No cash prizes or financial payouts
  • Substitutions only with comparable non-cash alternatives
  • Reward details shown before participation
